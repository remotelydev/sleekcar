## What “thin” means

A page is **thin** when it is short template copy: a brief lead plus the same repeated H2s (for example “Dlaczego warto?”, “Ceny i czas”, “Jak przebiega usługa?”, “Najczęstsze pytania”) without unique body, packages, process, or local detail. Structure is fine; the missing work is unique writing in Prismic.
